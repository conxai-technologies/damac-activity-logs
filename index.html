What each term means

Every material snag has exactly one outcome: it was deleted, it was changed (its description or type was edited, or it was switched to non-material), or it had no change. Priority and trade changes are counted separately, so a snag whose only change was its priority or trade counts as no change. Only changes made by the review team (MM and HA) count, and each snag is judged on where it ended up compared with how it was issued, so edits that were later undone do not count.
