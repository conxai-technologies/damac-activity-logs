Reading the page

  • Each colour is a different person, as in the source sheet: the MEP inspector, the MEP specialist, the QA inspector and whoever ran the capture. Times are the minutes each person logged, so a unit's recorded time is person-time, not time on site.
  • MEP general and QA functional are totals for the whole activity. Open one for its sub-tasks; Other functional is the total less the sub-tasks that were timed, i.e. the time they do not cover. Sub-tasks share the activity's colour, and the faint band behind them is the activity's own session.
  • Not recorded separately means the sheet logs 0 minutes or says so: the time is inside the activity total, and so inside Other functional. Activity pending means the sheet's row is blank.
  • Where the sheet leaves MEP specialist blank, it is shown as 30m, marked start and end time not logged. That time counts towards the unit's MEP and recorded time.
  • Per 100 m² is a unit’s recorded time divided by its floor area, so units of different sizes compare. Switch the chart between total time and per 100 m² with the buttons above it. Floor areas and bedrooms come from data/time-motion-units.csv.
  • Tests are not in the sheet. The flood test (30m, start and end time not logged): conducted by contractor, Verified by functional QA. It is not part of the unit totals. The static test (20m, start and end time not logged): conducted by functional QA. Its time is part of QA functional — a sub-task in its dropdown — and of the unit’s recorded time. The drain check (T240 1h 29m, T241 1h 00m and T242 1h 11m, from its logged start and end): conducted by MEP team. Its time is part of MEP general — a sub-task in its dropdown — and of the unit’s recorded time.
  • A ! marks minutes that do not agree with the clock times logged beside them, or a sub-task logged outside its activity. Hover it for the detail. Differences of 2 minutes or less are rounding and are not marked. Otherwise minutes are shown exactly as the sheet records them, and every total is built from them — nothing has been corrected.
