Reading the log

  • Duration is start to end less any recorded break. Manhours is that duration multiplied by the number of inspectors on site.
  • Processing time is elapsed pipeline time, not manned effort, so it is kept out of the manhours totals.
  • A ! marks a figure that does not agree with the times recorded beside it. Hover it for the arithmetic. Values are shown exactly as the sheet records them — nothing has been corrected.
  • — means the sheet records N/A; tbc means the work is scheduled but not yet done.
