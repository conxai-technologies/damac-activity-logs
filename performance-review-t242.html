Glossary

  • Recall — the percentage of in-scope manually logged snags captured by AI with a useful description, defining the correct repair work, correct trade and location.
  • Certain match — the AI snag fully describes the onsite snag. Likely match — the AI snag partly describes it. No match — no AI snag covers it.
  • Snag merged with another — the AI detected the onsite snag, but the detection is not a separate snag on the dashboard: it sits with another AI snag. The AI detection is shown, without a dashboard snag ID.
  • AI only — AI snags on the dashboard that no onsite snag covers. Deleted ones were deleted on the QA/QC board; the rest are additional true positive AI snags. Material / Non-material — the snag's tag on the dashboard; snags not tagged non-material count as material.
